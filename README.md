##  Complete Feature Breakdown

### 1. Dual-Profit Accounting (Macro vs. Micro Truth)
* **Total Store Net Cash Profit:** Shows the big picture across your entire business (Total Sales − All Ad Spend − Product Costs − Shipping Fees = Real Net Cash in the bank).
* **Channel Attributed P&L:** Shows the micro picture for each marketing channel:
  * **Meta Ads Profit:** (Meta Sales) − (Meta Ad Spend) − (Product Cost of Meta buyers) = **Meta Net Profit**.
  * **Google Ads Profit:** (Google Sales) − (Google Ad Spend) − (Product Cost of Google buyers) = **Google Net Profit**.
  * **Amazon Direct Profit:** (Amazon Sales) − (Amazon FBA Fees) − (Amazon PPC Ads) − (Product Cost) = **Amazon Net Profit**.
  * **Organic & Email (Klaviyo) Profit:** (Email & Direct Sales) − (Product Cost) = **Pure High-Margin Profit**.

---

### 2. Multi-Touch Attribution Resolver (Stops Ad Platforms from Lying)
* Facebook claims: *"We generated $10,000 in sales!"*
* Google claims: *"We generated $8,000 in sales!"*
* But your store only made **$12,000 total**. Both platforms are claiming credit for the exact same buyer.
* **What Prism Does:** Uses a 4-tier Click-ID (`fbclid`, `gclid`, `ttclid`) and UTM waterfall to eliminate double-counting and reveal your **true, real blended ROAS**.

---

### 3. SKU & Product Margin Matrix (Finds Hidden Loss-Makers)
* Calculates true net profit for every individual product after manufacturing cost (COGS), shipping fees, and refund rates.
* **The Benefit:** Instantly reveals "killer products" that have high return rates (e.g. 15% returns) so you can stop wasting ad budget on them and scale your high-margin best-sellers.

---

### 4. 30/60/90-Day Cohort Repurchase Matrix
* Answers the #1 investor question: *"What percentage of customers who bought in Month 1 come back to buy again in Month 2 and Month 3?"*
* Displays dynamic monthly retention curves so you know your exact recurring customer revenue.

---

### 5. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) & VIP Segmentation
* Automatically categorizes your customer base into **RFM Loyalty Tiers**:
  *  **Champion VIPs:** Customers with 4+ orders generating 50%+ of your store's total revenue.
  *  **Loyal Repeat Buyers:** Customers with 2–3 orders.
  *  **One-Time Buyers:** Customers who need re-engagement campaigns.
* Includes 1-click **Export VIP Segment CSV** for targeted SMS and email retention flows.

---

### 6. Inventory Runway & Stock-Out Predictor
* Tracks real-time daily sales burn velocity for every product SKU.
* Calculates the exact number of days left before best-sellers go out of stock, alerting you to send reorders to manufacturers before losing sales.

---

### 7. 24/7 AI Anomaly & Profit Leak Sentinel
* Automatically monitors your store 24 hours a day using statistical 7-day rolling Z-scores.
* **Instant Alerts:**
  * ⚠️ *CAC Surge Alert:* Pings your phone if Facebook ad costs spike by >20% above normal.
  * ⚠️ *ROAS Decay Warning:* Flags when an ad set drops below your break-even threshold.
  * ⚠️ *Return Spike Alert:* Flags products experiencing abnormal customer return rates.
